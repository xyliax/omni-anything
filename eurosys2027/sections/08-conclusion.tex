\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Periodic interaction exposes repeated opportunities to release a session's idle KV and restore it before the next tick.
Using those opportunities requires coordinating transfer time with the GPU space occupied by restoration destinations.
\systemname combines uniform phase assignment with per-session eviction and restoration planning over a finite horizon, followed by infrequent revisions as histories and session populations change.
The design seeks to increase service capacity while preserving retained history and meeting recurring soft deadlines.
