\paragraph{When offsets are not controllable.}
Offset control is the strongest of the three grades of timing information.
Where the application does not permit assignment, rhythm visibility still
supports demand estimation and preparation ahead of use; how much of the
benefit survives is an empirical question.

\paragraph{Degenerate conditions.}
Aperiodic input, absent idle intervals, short sessions, or insufficient host
capacity degrade the mechanisms to the on-demand path. These regions are
reported as measured (Q7). The quality trade-off among retention policies is
an application-side parameter; its effect on the applicable domain is
discussed on the same footing.

\paragraph{Layered limitations.}
Engineering limits (current trigger paths, unverified concurrency scenarios,
observation overhead) are stated separately from conditions inherent to the
method (a describable rhythm and usable idle intervals). Model errors and
uncovered dimensions are listed explicitly; the current prototype does not
decide the final research scope, and the effect of adding devices depends on
actual topology and contention rather than admitting a general judgment.
