# EuroSys 2027 Submission Checklist

Last verified: 2026-08-25, Asia/Shanghai.

Authoritative source: [EuroSys 2027 Call for Papers](https://2027.eurosys.org/cfp.html). The official CFP and submission system override this local summary if they change.

## Important Dates

- Spring cycle: closed.
- Fall title and abstract registration: 2026-09-17, anywhere on Earth.
- Fall full paper submission: 2026-09-24, anywhere on Earth.
- Fall reviews available: 2027-01-06.
- Fall author response: 2027-01-08.
- Fall notification: 2027-01-29.
- Fall camera-ready: 2027-03-05.

## Format

- [ ] PDF submission.
- [ ] At most 12 pages of technical content.
- [ ] References may use additional pages without a fixed limit.
- [ ] Supplementary material, if any, is uploaded separately; the main paper is self-contained and stands on its own.
- [ ] A4 or US Letter paper size.
- [ ] All text and figures fit within a 178 × 229 mm (7 × 9 inch) block.
- [ ] Two columns separated by at least 8 mm (0.33 inch).
- [ ] All text, including figures and captions, is at least 10pt on at least 12pt leading.
- [ ] Graphs and figures are readable in grayscale without magnification.
- [ ] All pages are numbered.
- [ ] The SIGPLAN ACM template has not been modified to compress content.

## Double-Blind Review

- [ ] Author names and affiliations are absent from the PDF and metadata.
- [ ] Acknowledgments are omitted.
- [ ] Self-citations are written in the third person.
- [ ] Repository, artifact and project links are anonymous and their contents do not reveal identity.
- [ ] Screenshots, plots, logs and paths contain no names, usernames, institutions or domains.
- [ ] Concurrent under-review related work is cited anonymously and handled according to the CFP.

## Research and Publication Policy

- [ ] The submission is original and is not simultaneously submitted elsewhere.
- [ ] Any related workshop paper is uploaded and its additional material is explained.
- [ ] Each author is listed on at most three EuroSys 2027 submissions in the selected cycle.
- [ ] ACM authorship, plagiarism, conflict-of-interest, peer-review and human-participant policies have been checked.
- [ ] All uses of AI tools are fully disclosed as required by the EuroSys 2027 CFP and current ACM policy; `ai-use-log.md` is complete.
- [ ] At least one author is prepared for the required full registration if accepted.
- [ ] ORCID IDs and ACM Open/APC implications are checked before camera-ready production.

## Content Quality Criteria from the CFP

- [ ] Novelty is defended against the closest work.
- [ ] Significance and systems-community relevance are explicit.
- [ ] The solution is compelling and technically correct.
- [ ] Evaluation rigorously shows benefits and limitations.
- [ ] Advantages over prior work are measured or otherwise substantiated.
- [ ] The paper is clear, self-contained and does not depend on optional supplementary material.

## Final Mechanical Audit

- [ ] `make check` passes.
- [ ] PDF page count and the technical-content/reference boundary are inspected manually.
- [ ] PDF is reviewed at 100% zoom and in grayscale.
- [ ] Fonts are embedded.
- [ ] No draft placeholder, `TBD`, TODO, line-note, hidden annotation or identifying PDF metadata remains.
- [ ] The official CFP and HotCRP instructions are rechecked on the submission day.
