# Twelve-Page Budget

EuroSys 2027 allows at most 12 pages of technical content, with unlimited additional pages for references. This is a planning budget, not permission to alter the official template.

旧的分节预算基于过期章节结构，已于 2026-09-13 清理；重建时按 docs/PAPER.md 的章节骨架重新分配 12 页技术内容（参考量级：Intro ≈1.5、Background/Motivation ≈1.5、Design ≈3、Implementation ≈1、Evaluation ≈3.75、Related Work ≈0.75、Discussion+Conclusion ≈0.5）。

If the draft exceeds this budget, cut scope or prose. Do not change font size, line spacing, margins, column gap, caption size or class internals.
