# One-sentence conclusion per figure

Generated by results/make_plots.py from the CSVs in results/. (Numbering:
S1=density, S2=cancellation, S3=injection shock — renumbered 2026-08.)

## S1_density.png

Beat-phase scatter is the dominant throughput loss: at N=12 random phase batches only 1.5 sequences per step against 6.5 when beats are aligned, spending 5.0x the GPU time for identical work; the beat deadline is never the binding limit inside the KV-feasible range.

## S2_cancellation.png

Interrupts turn tool prefill into pure waste that the current system never reclaims: at L=8192 with a 40% interrupt prior, 16384 prefill tokens are computed for content the user already invalidated, and at peak 24% of the KV the engine is holding is known-dead context, resident a mean of 36s -- it stays in the context that every later beat attends over.

## S3_injection.png

A single session absorbs a whole splice up to L=5120 tokens; the first deadline miss appears at L=6144 and only when the result lands late in the beat (offset 470ms of 480), so L* is phase-dependent, not a single number.

## S3_timeline.png

The shock is one beat wide: the splice delays the beat it lands on and the next beat is already back to baseline, so the damage does not accumulate for a single session.
